I am a Machine Learning researcher interested in Continual Learning, Interpretability, Network Pruning, Long-Term Time Series Forecasting, Medical Imaging
and Reasoning in Large Language Models.

My recent work focuses on:
- correlation-aware transformer architectures
- continual segmentation learning
- multimodal representation learning
- efficient sequence modeling

I am particularly interested in developing efficient and interpretable
deep learning systems for real-world sequential and multimodal data.
